# Community adventure content

The main Oriental Mindoro route covers all 15 municipalities and the city through five missions each:

1. History
2. Barangay count
3. Ways of living
4. Traditions (including clearly labeled local origin stories)
5. Environment

Only aggregate barangay counts enter the app: 426 across the province, from PSA municipality/city tables dated 31 July 2025. No barangay names, directory, search index or full research tables are included in the app data.

Tourism spots, cultural-site visits and festivals are reserved for future separate adventures. Existing place backgrounds remain visual identifiers; lessons and recaps no longer promote attraction visits. Mission IDs, saved discoveries and earned badges remain compatible with existing progress.

Each mission has a sourced discovery and a matching pre-run lesson. Optional recaps reinforce the barangay count; journal pages synthesize the same five topics. Origin stories are explicitly distinguished from established historical events. Community-specific practices are attributed to their bearers rather than assigned to all residents.
