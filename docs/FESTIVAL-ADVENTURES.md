# Oriental Mindoro festival adventures

The Festivals departure group has 18 separate destinations and 72 runner missions (four per festival). Every city and municipality has a festival route. Calapan City also has three province-wide celebrations. Each route has an AI-generated photographic-style PNG background and three transparent PNG game assets: a runner character, a collectible item, and a commemorative passport icon. The images are illustrative reconstructions, not photographs of the festivals or official costumes and objects.

The festival names and locations follow the [Oriental Mindoro tourism festival directory](https://www.travelorientalmindoro.ph/Festivals/). The province's [2018 Facts and Figures](https://ppdo.ormindoro.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/FACTS_and_FIGURES_2018.pdf) was used to cross-check municipality coverage. The tourism directory uses Kaaldawan-Iraya and MASAYAW for Puerto Galera and San Teodoro; the older planning document lists Kaaldawan Mangyan and Coco. This pack follows the current tourism directory. Mission titles, objectives, collectibles, guides, and artwork are original game interpretations, not descriptions of official festival activities.

Each festival route begins unlocked at mission 1. Completing a mission opens the next. Completing all four adds its stamp to the existing passport. The original 15 community campaigns remain separate and retain their progress and stamps.

To rebuild the catalog, run `python scripts/build-festival-adventures.py` from the repository root. The final photographic backgrounds and transparent game assets are kept in `public/assets/festival/`.
