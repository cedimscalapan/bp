# Heritage journey rewards

The home screen recommends the next unfinished, unlocked Oriental Mindoro mission, prioritizing communities the traveler has started. It displays campaign completion, communities visited through successful missions, and earned stamps. When all 180 missions are complete, it offers a completion message instead of an invalid next mission.

Missions now includes six permanent achievements: one clear, five different clears, clears in three communities, three gold medals, a passport stamp, and fifteen different clears. Each has a visible goal and one-time coin reward. Existing saved completions qualify; repeated runs do not inflate the number of different missions cleared. Other regions do not count toward these Oriental Mindoro achievements. Previously earned stamps retain their established migration behavior.

Reward claims are checked against current saved progress at the time of claiming. The optional `achievementClaims` string array is included in backups. Old saves need no migration and retain all existing progress. Failed missions do not add completion records or qualify for completion achievements.

Runner play celebrates pickup streaks at 5, 10, and 20 without changing score rules or interrupting movement. The brief overlay ignores touch input and respects reduced-motion preferences.

Result cards show the run's medal, the next score threshold, traveler XP gained, level-up announcements, and newly earned milestones. A successful run can lead directly into the next mission's Buddy selection. Failed runs retain their collected coins under the existing economy; their discovery card is not displayed as a successful discovery.

This adds no time-limited streak obligation, purchases, accounts, analytics, investigations, or new learning minigames. Achievement rewards remain available until claimed. Human playtesting is still needed to evaluate pacing, enjoyment, and the existing score thresholds across ages.
