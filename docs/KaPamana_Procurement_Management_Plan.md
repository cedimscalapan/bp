# KA-PAMANA Procurement Management Plan

**Project:** KA-PAMANA: A Mobile Application for Interactive Learning and Preservation of Oriental Mindoro's Culture and Heritage  
**Team:** Mathew Andrei F. Abao; John Christopher C. Araño; Mica V. Palpallatoc  
**Class:** BSIT 4-5  
**Version and date:** 1.0 · 17 November 2026

## Purpose and approach

This plan controls purchases, subscriptions, licenses, and borrowed equipment needed to develop, test, and present KA-PAMANA. The team will use suitable existing or no-cost resources first. A paid commitment requires the approval required by the team's funding arrangement and school rules. No price or supplier is assumed in this plan.

## Planned acquisitions

| Need | Acquisition approach | Acceptance check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Android test phone and accessories | Use a team-owned or borrowed device; purchase only if a suitable device is unavailable. | Current APK installs, opens, and completes the agreed demo sequence. |
| Internet and mobile data | Prepare a primary connection and an independent backup for account access and the live presentation. | Both connections support sign-in and the planned demonstration. |
| Supabase or other cloud capacity | Use the configured service within its approved limits; consider a paid plan only when measured use or school requirements justify it. | Account access, content delivery, and player-save synchronization meet the agreed test cases. |
| Media, fonts, and presentation materials | Reuse owned or properly licensed assets; buy or commission only missing items. Print required copies on the specified paper. | Usage rights and attribution are recorded; images, audio, slides, video, and print copies are usable offline where needed. |

## Procurement procedure and responsibility

1. A team member records the need, quantity, estimated cost in PHP, required date, and acceptance check before obtaining an item.
2. The team compares suitable options on total cost, compatibility, support, licensing, and data handling. For material purchases, it records multiple quotes where practical.
3. The team obtains the required funding and school approval **before** ordering or subscribing. A team member keeps the approval, receipt, license, and service details in the procurement log.
4. On receipt, the requesting member checks the item against the acceptance criteria. The team records any defect, refund, replacement, or subscription change.

## Budget, records, and change control

The procurement log records item, supplier, estimate, approved amount, actual amount, purchase date, receipt or license, responsible member, and status. The team reviews the log before each development or presentation milestone. Any unplanned purchase, recurring fee, or increase beyond the approved amount requires a new approval before commitment. Account credentials remain in controlled team access, not in the public repository.

## Main risks and responses

| Risk | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Device or connection is unavailable | Use a tested loaner or backup connection; keep the APK, slides, and narrated demo video locally. |
| Service cost rises or limits are reached | Review actual usage before upgrading; keep local progress and a recoverable backup. |
| Asset rights are unclear | Replace the asset or obtain written permission before use; keep source and license records. |
