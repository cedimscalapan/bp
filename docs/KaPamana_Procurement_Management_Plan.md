# KA-PAMANA Procurement Management Plan

**Project:** KA-PAMANA: A Mobile Application for Interactive Learning and Preservation of Oriental Mindoro's Culture and Heritage  
**Team:** Mathew Andrei F. Abao; John Christopher C. Araño; Mica V. Palpallatoc  
**Class:** BSIT 4-5  
**Version and date:** 2.0 · 17 November 2026

## 1. Purpose and scope

This plan explains how the team will identify, obtain, check, and record the goods and services needed to build, test, and present KA-PAMANA. It applies to purchases, subscriptions, commissioned work, licensed assets, printing, and borrowed equipment. The team will use suitable existing or no-cost resources first. A paid commitment will proceed only after the funding owner and any required school authority approve its cost and purpose. This plan does not assume a budget, supplier, or price that the team has not confirmed.

## 2. Roles and decision authority

| Role | Responsibility | Decision or record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Requesting team member | Defines the need, quantity, required date, estimated cost, and acceptance check; tests the delivered item. | Submits a request and signs the receipt or test result. |
| Team coordinator | Checks that an existing resource cannot reasonably meet the need; gathers options and maintains the procurement log. | Recommends an option and tracks its status. |
| Funding owner and adviser, when required | Reviews affordability, school rules, and any recurring or rights-related commitment. | Gives written approval before an order, payment, or subscription. |

One member may fill more than one team role, but the purchase request and approval must remain distinguishable in the record. The team will assign a coordinator and identify the funding owner before its first paid commitment.

## 4. Request, selection, and approval process

1. **Raise a request.** The requesting member records the item or service, purpose, specification, quantity, needed date, estimated cost in Philippine pesos, funding source, and a measurable acceptance check. The request states whether the need is one-time or recurring.
2. **Check available resources.** The coordinator checks team-owned, borrowed, school-provided, and no-cost options. For a material purchase or subscription, the team compares suitable providers where practical and keeps the quotes or dated price references.
3. **Evaluate the options.** The team considers total cost over the expected project period, compatibility with the Android app and existing workflow, availability by the required date, support or replacement terms, usage rights, privacy, and data export. The chosen option and reason are entered in the log.
4. **Obtain approval.** The funding owner, and adviser if school rules require it, approves the exact item, amount or spending ceiling, and any renewal obligation in writing. The team must not order, subscribe, or promise payment before approval. A quote that exceeds the approved ceiling requires a revised approval.
5. **Place and track the order.** A designated member places the approved order using a team-controlled account when appropriate. The coordinator records the supplier, order date, expected delivery, actual cost, receipt, and license or service terms. Access credentials are stored privately rather than in the public repository.
6. **Receive and accept.** The requester checks quantity, condition, functionality, and rights against the request. A defect or mismatch is recorded and resolved through correction, replacement, refund, or rejection before the item is marked accepted.

## 5. Budget, payment, and change control

The coordinator maintains one procurement log with request ID, owner, item, supplier, estimated and approved amounts, actual amount, dates, status, receipt or invoice, license or contract link, acceptance result, and renewal or end date. The log is reviewed before each major test and presentation milestone. Receipts and approvals are retained in the team's controlled project records.

The team tracks committed costs as well as amounts already paid so that a subscription or pending order is not overlooked. An unplanned item, change in supplier, increase above the approved ceiling, or new recurring fee requires a revised request and approval before the team commits. The team records who will cancel, renew, or transfer each subscription at project closeout. Refunds and unused materials are also entered in the log.

## 7. Supplier, license, and data requirements

For every paid service or media asset, the coordinator records the supplier's name, scope of use, payment terms, renewal date, cancellation route, and support contact. Images, music, fonts, and commissioned graphics must permit the intended app and presentation use; attribution or redistribution conditions are followed. The team should keep an editable or source copy where the license allows it. If a service processes user data, the team checks access controls, data export, retention, and deletion options before use. Any rights or privacy uncertainty is escalated to the adviser before the asset or service is adopted.

## 8. Main risks and responses

| Risk | Early indicator | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Device or network fails during the demo | Installation or connection fails at rehearsal. | Test a loaner phone and backup connection; keep the APK, slides, and narrated demo locally. |
| Service use exceeds limits or planned cost | Usage or invoices approach the approved ceiling. | Review actual usage, reduce avoidable demand, and obtain approval before an upgrade. |
| Asset rights or quality are inadequate | Source, permission, or technical specification is missing. | Replace the asset or obtain permission; check the revised file before content freeze. |
| Delivery or printing is late | Supplier misses the agreed date. | Order ahead of the deadline, follow up on status, and use a tested local or digital alternative. |
