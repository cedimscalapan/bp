# KaPamana: Oriental Mindoro research pack

Research date: **20 October 2026**. Coverage: **Calapan City and all 14 municipalities**. Festivals are excluded.

The complete content is in [Oriental-Mindoro-content-pack.json](Oriental-Mindoro-content-pack.json). It contains 15 introductions, 30 collectible proposals, 75 sourced discovery cards (five per location), 15 visitor challenges, 15 optional recaps with correct answers, and 15 journal drafts. Every location uses the existing app destination ID. Source records include publisher, title, link, evidence type, scope and access date.

These are web-researched drafts, not content already added to the app. `publishInApp` is false. A source link is evidence of a published claim, not permission to reproduce its artwork, poetry or recordings. The pack separates documentary claims, legends, oral accounts and original game proposals.

## What the research changes about the current game

- Replace the idea of taking shells, live fish, cave material or fragments of ruins with photo, observation or illustrated keepsake tokens. [Green Fins guidance](https://greenfins.net/blog/top-tips-agents-operators/?cp=3) supports leaving marine-life souvenirs in place.
- Product-making can explain how local people earn a living; it need not be called an ancient tradition. The documented producer examples support better content than assigning every place a generic coconut or fish.
- Keep the Pinamalayan clay-pot sprite only as a landmark souvenir until a separate pottery tradition is documented. A photograph of a monument does not prove the existence of that craft locally.
- Do not assign a generic Mangyan language, clothing, motif or story to all places. The Puerto Galera and Mansalay records identify different cultural connections; neither grants permission to copy designs or verse.
- A quiz is optional. The journal opens after the fifth mission; a wrong recap answer does not erase gameplay rewards.

## Source-quality findings and unresolved issues

| Issue | Research decision |
|---|---|
| Socorro creation date conflicts between summaries | Use 22 June 1963 from [RA 3609](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/25043), corroborated by the municipal website. |
| San Rafael Cave listing gives both seven and ten chambers | Exclude chamber counts. Do not repeat its generic access, safety or fee assurances. |
| Mount Halcon national rankings differ between tourism/history pages | Omit national ranking. Teach the Baco association instead. |
| Kuta chronology differs between local pages | Prefer the eighteenth-century PIA interpretation for the draft; seek a site/NHCP review before release. |
| Calapan early capital-transfer chronology differs | Omit the disputed dates. The cityhood plebiscite is a separate, clearly cited claim. |
| Naujan history infers ancestry from imported ceramics | Reject the inference. Trade objects alone do not establish a population's ancestry. |
| Tourism portal has generic free-entry labels, rounded statistics and placeholder-looking contacts | Do not use them for operational guidance or current demographic claims. |
| Older reports discuss livelihoods and projects | Frame as documented examples, not guarantees that an enterprise currently operates or offers visits. |
| Heritage-center school-teaching claim available through a search excerpt; direct retrieval failed | Retain only in the source ledger and verification gaps; exclude from the 75 publishable-candidate discovery texts. |
| Many named photo references are not independently geolocated | Do not name an exact waterfall, beach, boardwalk or cave from appearance alone. |

## What is still missing

Web research supports introductions, historical/identity discoveries and livelihood-based proposals for every location. It does **not** establish a unique, approved living tradition for every municipality. The JSON explicitly records those gaps rather than filling them with invented material.

Before educational publication, ask municipal cultural/tourism staff and relevant community contributors to check the drafts, names and interpretations. Obtain separate agreements for specific craft designs, Indigenous writing, verses, translations, narration and photographs. No messages have been sent to those offices or communities.

Conservation challenges are proposed learning mechanics. Where no specific guidance source is attached, they are editorial suggestions awaiting site review, not statements of local law or visitor policy. Do not reward touching wildlife, harvesting plants, entering a closed trail or handling hazardous debris.

The next implementation step is to approve a small pilot set, then map its five discoveries to the existing five missions. Store read/unlocked discovery IDs with backward-compatible defaults. Keep the research outside the shipped catalog until reviewed.
