# KA-PAMANA presentation runbook

**Project:** KA-PAMANA: A Mobile Application for Interactive Learning and Preservation of Oriental Mindoro's Culture and Heritage  
**Presenters:** Abao, Mathew Andrei F.; Araño, John Christopher C.; Palpallatoc, Mica V.  
**Class:** BSIT 4-5

## Before leaving for the presentation

- Print the Chapters 1 and 3 document on **8.5 × 13 inch long bond paper**. Print the number of copies required for each subject.
- Copy the PowerPoint and MP4 to the presentation laptop and a USB drive. Confirm that the MP4 plays **offline** from the local copy.
- Download the latest successful **KaPamana-Android** artifact from [the Android build workflow](https://github.com/cedimscalapan/bp/actions/workflows/android-apk.yml). Unzip it and install `app-debug.apk` on the Android demo phone before presentation day.
- Sign in with a dedicated test account. Avoid showing its password or personal email on the projector.
- Open a festival route, play the chosen mission once, and confirm the Buddy and guide roles. Prepare one saved tracing image.
- Charge the phone and laptop. Bring a charger or power bank, connector, and any needed display adapter.
- Provide your **own primary internet connection** and a **backup connection**, such as a second phone hotspot. Test both with the presentation laptop and demo phone.

## Live demo sequence

| Time | Action | What to show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:30 | Open the app | Account sign-in, traveler name, and chosen Buddy. |
| 0:30–1:15 | Open Adventures | Oriental Mindoro Festivals, one municipality or city, guide artwork, and four missions. |
| 1:15–2:15 | Play a mission | Buddy remains the runner; collect an item and avoid a hazard. |
| 2:15–2:45 | Show result and passport | Stars, rewards, route progress, and festival stamp. |
| 2:45–3:45 | Open Learn | Enter a word, inspect the script rendering, trace it, save the image, and open Share. |
| 3:45–4:15 | Show cloud progress | Point to account-linked backup and explain that offline changes sync on reconnection. |

The converter renders a Latin spelling for script practice. It does not translate a Filipino word into the Hanunó'o language. Festival art is generated representation, not event photography.

## If the live demo is interrupted

1. Switch from the primary connection to the backup hotspot and reopen the app.
2. If the phone cannot connect, show the already installed app's offline screens and local save.
3. Play the local `KaPamana_Actual_App_Demo.mp4` from the laptop. The video lasts **90 seconds** and does not need internet. The earlier `KaPamana_Feature_Video_Motion.mp4` remains a backup.
4. Continue with the slide deck and answer questions using the printed documentation.

## Narrated video

The 90-second video has its own Filipino-English voiceover, music, and on-screen titles. Play it with the presentation laptop's sound enabled. The complete voiceover text is in `Video_Narration_Script.md`; presenters can introduce the video and then let it play without speaking over it.
