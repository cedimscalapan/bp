# KA-PAMANA phone recording shot list

Record the **actual app on an Android phone in portrait orientation**. Keep the original MP4 files. A 1080 × 1920 recording at 30 frames per second is ideal; a phone's normal screen recorder setting is fine. Turn on Do Not Disturb and hide notifications. Do not record a password, personal email, or other private account details.

| File to send | Record this action | Useful length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `01_buddy.mp4` | Open Buddies, tap a Buddy, and show that it is selected. | 5–8 seconds |
| `02_festivals.mp4` | Open Adventures, select **Oriental Mindoro Festivals**, open one festival, and show its guide and mission list. | 8–12 seconds |
| `03_runner.mp4` | Show the boarding pass with your chosen Buddy, then gameplay with lane changes and a festival keepsake collection. Record a full attempt if needed; I can trim it. | 15–30 seconds of usable action |
| `04_result_passport.mp4` | Show the mission result, then open Passport and show the festival stamp or progress. | 8–12 seconds |
| `05_hanunoo.mp4` | In Learn, type a word, show its script rendering, trace it, save the drawing, and open **Save image** or **Share to Stories**. | 15–25 seconds |
| `06_cloud_optional.mp4` | Show a signed-in traveler's progress after reopening the app, or a restore on a second phone if this has been tested. Keep account identifiers hidden. | 5–10 seconds |

You can send clips 1–5 separately. Clip 6 is optional. No voice recording is required; the presentation video can use music and on-screen captions. Leave a second or two before and after each action so edits are clean. Avoid changing orientation while recording.
