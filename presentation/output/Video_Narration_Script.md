# KA-PAMANA video narration

The 90-second app demo includes this narrated story over the team's actual phone recording. The voice is an AI-generated Filipino-English narrator.

> Every place has a story. In Oriental Mindoro, those stories live in festivals, landmarks, and the writing traditions of its people. We created KaPamana because we wanted learning about that heritage to feel close, active, and worth sharing.
>
> Instead of simply reading a page, travelers begin an adventure. They choose a Buddy and run through Calapan, collecting keepsakes and earning rewards. A question at the finish connects the game to the meaning of the place. The heritage journal invites them to keep exploring.
>
> Travelers can pick a companion for the next journey. In the learning studio, they follow the shape of Hanunó’o characters with a finger. They can type a word to see a character-by-character spelling guide, then practice each shape at their own pace.
>
> Their tracing becomes an image they can save and share through the phone’s apps. This is the real story card exported from KaPamana. We built the app to help culture stay present in everyday life: something to discover, remember, and pass on.

The video shows the Android share sheet with Facebook and Instagram options. The supplied recording does not show a completed social media post.
